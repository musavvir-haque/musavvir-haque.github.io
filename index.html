Introduction

About Me

I am an aspiring PhD researcher and B.Sc. graduate (2026) in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from Jashore University of Science and Technology (JUST), Bangladesh. My research bridges computational materials science, machine learning, and signal processing.

My primary focus is on plasma–material interactions in fusion-relevant tungsten, ML-accelerated materials modelling (DFT/MD surrogates), ion implantation physics in semiconductor substrates, and real-time wearable biosignal processing using advanced signal decomposition.

Beyond research, I served as Chair of the IEEE Signal Processing Society JUST Student Branch Chapter, where I organised technical events, the SigMa Mentorship Programme 2026, and workshops fostering undergraduate research culture.

Journal Article Under Review

[J1] Hydrogen Implantation and Diffusion in BCC Tungsten: A Systematic Molecular Dynamics Study over 5–300 eV and 300–1500 K

Musavvir Haque, Md. Amzad Hossain

Submitted to Fusion Engineering and Design (Q2) · Supervisor: Md. Amzad Hossain

Large-scale classical MD investigation of hydrogen implantation, retention, diffusion, and radiation damage in plasma-exposed BCC tungsten. Uses the Bonny EAM/alloy W–H–He potential in LAMMPS across 1,000 simulations. Identifies non-monotonic retention behaviour, implantation depth saturation, Arrhenius diffusion, and cascade-dominated high-energy regimes relevant to ITER and DEMO.

Journal Article Under Revision

[J2] Gatekeeper-Constrained Dual-Stage Random Forest Surrogate for Multi-Output Ion Implantation Prediction across Si, SiC, and GaAs

Md. A. Hossain, Musavvir Haque (2nd Author), et al.

Submitted to Computational Materials Science (Q1)

A novel dual-stage machine learning surrogate incorporating a gatekeeper classifier to constrain prediction scope, enabling rapid and accurate multi-output ion implantation modelling across three semiconductor substrates (Si, SiC, GaAs). Achieves strong predictive performance while maintaining physical interpretability, offering a computationally efficient alternative to Monte Carlo / TRIM simulations.

03
B.Sc. Thesis Submitted

[T1] Multi-Scale DFT–MD–ML Modelling on Hydrogen Isotope Retention and Oxide Dynamics in Tungsten with Plasma Exposure for Fusion Reactor

Musavvir Haque

B.Sc. Submitted Thesis, JUST · Supervisor: Md. Tariquzzaman, Assistant Professor

Multiscale framework integrating DFT/MD-derived physical trends with machine learning for hydrogen isotope retention and oxide-layer evolution in plasma-exposed tungsten. XGBoost regressor: MAE = 0.017 eV, R² = 0.88 for binding energy; MAE = 0.91%, R² = 0.87 for crack density. Validated against TDS experiments and WEST tokamak observations.
